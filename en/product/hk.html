Kapton Film Heater

Ultra-thin (≤0.254mm) film heater with resistance wire or etched foil between polyimide (Kapton) layers. Flexible for curved surfaces and custom shapes.

Features

  • Thin, flexible design with excellent heat resistance and electrical insulation
  • Custom-manufactured in various sizes and shapes
  • Easy installation with PSA (pressure-sensitive adhesive) option
  • Stable performance in high-temperature and chemical environments

Applications

Temperature control in semiconductor equipment and vacuum chambers; medical and laboratory equipment; preheating of aerospace and defense electronics; low-temperature heaters for batteries, optics and precision instruments; temperature stabilization of sensors and measuring devices.
