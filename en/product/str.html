Industrial Process RTD

High-accuracy industrial RTD based on the resistance change of a metal conductor. Except for the 30mm tip, it bends up to 5× its outer diameter.

Features

  • More accurate than thermocouple-type sensors
  • Flexible except the sensing tip — easy to install in pipe curves and narrow sections
  • Explosion-proof / Weather-proof (IP68) heads
  • Bolt · Movable · DIN connector · Compact types
  • Energization lockout sensors — Teflon lining / Teflon fitting

Applications

Temperature measurement inside piping and process lines; monitoring in power plants, chemical plants and semiconductor equipment; control in pressure vessels, heat exchangers and cooling systems; hygienic process lines in pharmaceutical and food facilities.
