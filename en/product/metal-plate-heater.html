Metal Plate Heater

Large-area heater with heating elements and thermal sensors embedded in a precision-machined metal base for uniform heating of wafers and substrates.

Features

  • Uniform temperature distribution and fast response
  • High flatness and heat resistance in high-vacuum, high-temperature environments
  • Conductive and insulated configurations
  • Integrated cooling lines, temperature sensors and electrodes — tailored to your equipment
  • 8-layer stack: metal base · graphite · mica · 1T mica heater · heat-resistant silicone

Applications

Semiconductor wafer heating equipment, display manufacturing equipment, vacuum chambers and plasma systems, laser and precision processing equipment, and other equipment requiring uniform heat distribution.
