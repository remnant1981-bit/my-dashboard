Industrial Process Thermocouples

Industrial thermocouples using precious or base-metal conductors to measure from -200℃ up to +2200℃. MgO-filled sheath for excellent insulation and pressure resistance.

Features

  • Measurement range -200℃ (low) to +2200℃ (ultra-high)
  • From general industrial to explosion-proof models for hazardous areas
  • High-purity magnesium oxide (MgO) filled sheath
  • For semiconductors (metal chuck) · industry (quick connector) · reactors (multi-point)

Applications

High-temperature facilities such as power plants, steel mills and chemical plants; gas turbines, boilers, incinerators and semiconductor processes; monitoring in explosive areas and special environments.
