Stator Winding RTD

Thin RTD inserted between stator slots to monitor winding overheating in motors and generators. The whole body length senses temperature, giving an averaged reading.

Features

  • Platinum, copper or nickel elements — thin and non-inductive
  • Vibration resistant and withstands high voltage
  • Sensing element extends through most of the body length to eliminate tip-sensing errors (ANSI C50-10-1990, Sec. 5.2)
  • Gas/Air type, Slot type (large generators) · Chip type, Drawing type (medium & large motors)
  • FX (Single·Dual) and TX (Single·Dual) types, fully custom-made

Applications

Temperature monitoring and overheat protection in large and medium motors, generators and other rotating machinery.
