Industrial Process Thermostats

Bimetal-based protective temperature switch that cuts power or starts a cooling fan when equipment overheats.

Features

  • Stable switching based on the bimetal principle
  • Normally Closed (default) / Normally Open (selectable)
  • IP45 water resistance (manufacturer guaranteed)
  • THREAD · TUBE · THREAD-Connector types

Applications

Temperature control of electric motors, battery chargers and transformers; protection of power supplies, heating pads, fluorescent lights and ballasts; OA equipment, solenoids and LED lighting.
