Surface Temperature Detector

Measures the surface temperature of pipes, tanks, batteries and HV transformers to prevent damage from overheating or freezing of internal media or coils.

Features

  • Built-in platinum RTD or thermocouple for fast response
  • Compact, moisture-resistant design for quick mounting on various surfaces
  • Measures the average temperature along its length to minimize point-measurement errors
  • Conforms to curved surfaces; optimized for motor/generator winding sensing
  • Chip type · Drawing type · High-temp (Mica / PI)

Applications

Temperature measurement in confined spaces such as motor coil ends, bio and semiconductor equipment, batteries, pressure vessels, aerospace, defense and medical devices. Used for overheat prevention, cooling control and thermal distribution monitoring.
