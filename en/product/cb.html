Cable Bushing

Flame-proof connector for safely connecting power, sensor and signal cables between internal and external explosion-proof equipment. CBC is designed for low temperatures down to -55℃.

Features

  • High heat-resistant insulation and precision-sealed design
  • CBC model specialized for low-temperature service down to -55℃
  • Customizable cable diameters and materials
  • Screw-mounted installation

Applications

Internal wiring in junction boxes within explosion-proof areas; power and sensor connections for motors and industrial equipment; power plants, terminals, storage tanks and other hazardous environments.
