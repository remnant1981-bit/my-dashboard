Glass Bushing / Feedthrough

Glass-to-metal sealed feedthrough for power and sensors that maintains high vacuum and airtightness — rated down to -253℃ for liquid hydrogen.

Features

  • Special glass-to-metal seal for high vacuum tightness
  • Cryogenic service (LNG, LH₂)
  • Custom current rating, pin arrangement, insulation and housing metal
  • Threaded / weld-in / flange types

Applications

LNG / liquid hydrogen (LH₂) tanks and terminals; power plants, vacuum systems and vacuum pumps; semiconductor and battery manufacturing; sensor feedthroughs for gas and cryogenic equipment; sealed connections for power and signal transmission.
