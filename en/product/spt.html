Pressure transmitter integrating a high-stability piezoresistive sensor, specialized circuitry and a high-strength stainless steel housing. Intrinsically safe and explosion-proof versions available.

  • Various threaded ports and electrical connection options
  • Robust durability for harsh environments
  • Differential · High precision · Display · Basic · Compact types
  • Angular connector A / M12×1 (4-pin) plug / cable type

Industrial automation and process control, semiconductor automation equipment, refrigeration and compressor control systems, automotive and mobility.
